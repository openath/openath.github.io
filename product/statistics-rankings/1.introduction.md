## __1__ Introduction

OpenTrack's Statistics & Rankings turns the results captured across our Competition Management and Federation Management Systems into national rankings, athlete progressions and open, shareable statistics.

We provide national rankings and athlete profiles for our federation clients, covering all age groups and all registered athletes. Every result is tied back to a unique athlete ID from our <a href="/product/open-reference-data.html">Open Reference Database</a>, so rankings stay accurate as athletes move between clubs, countries and events.
