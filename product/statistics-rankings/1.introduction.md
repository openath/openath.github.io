## __1__ Introduction

OpenTrack's Statistics &amp; Rankings combines results captured across our Competition Management System with federation-inputted data into a searchable, comparable body of statistics for your federation — national lists, athlete progressions, and open statistics anyone can use.

Every performance is tied to a single, consistent athlete identity, so the numbers stay accurate as athletes move between clubs, countries, and events.
