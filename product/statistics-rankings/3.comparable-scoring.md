## __3__ Comparable scoring

A 100m and a shot put can't be compared on raw numbers alone, so OpenTrack scores every performance. Our scoring calculations mean you have comparable values at your fingertips spanning track, field, road, and multi-events, so the best at your competition is a question we can actually answer.
