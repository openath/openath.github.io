## __2__ The performance list

Every notable result across your federation, in one filterable list on your federation's `/stats` page. It's the day-to-day tool for statisticians, selectors, and anyone following the season.

Every athlete has their own page too, gathering their performances in one place alongside links to their profiles on World Athletics, Power of 10, and Tilastopaja.
