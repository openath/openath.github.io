## __4__ Data sources

Your federation's statistics are built from two streams. Results scored in OpenTrack's <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/product/competition-management.html">Competition Management System</a> flow in on their own, rebuilding incrementally so a large championship doesn't mean a long wait for the lists to catch up. Everything else — other competitions, overseas results, historical marks — statisticians add by hand.
</div>
