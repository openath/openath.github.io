## __6__ Data and access

The data in OpenTrack's Statistics & Rankings module is yours, and every filtered list exports to a spreadsheet, and JSON endpoints cover event combinations, performances, and top-ten lists for anyone building on the data.

Access follows the sport's lines of responsibility: the statistics APIs are open to your in-country statisticians and federation administrators, while non-personal data is public by default, in keeping with our open approach to data.
