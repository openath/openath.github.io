## __2__ Validation and Assurance

Behind every well-run federation is high-quality data. OpenTrack keeps the picture accurate all year round — who is licensed, which club they belong to, and when and where they're competing.

#### Data Visibility
Not everyone should see the same things. Access runs from federation administrators down to individual athletes, so officials, club representatives, coaches, statisticians, and athletes each see exactly what they need — and nothing they shouldn't. Clubs manage their own members and entries, but the federation sets the rules they work within, and every change is visible centrally as it happens.

#### Licence Management
Athlete licences are an important revenue source for federations, and managing them without a proper system is slow and error-prone.

On OpenTrack, issuing and renewing licences is straightforward, whether for individual athletes or whole clubs at once. Because licences live alongside competition data, an athlete's eligibility is checked automatically before they step onto the track — there is nothing to reconcile on the day.

On-demand and scheduled reports give you the clearest possible picture of who is in the sport, and for smaller countries, season-long bib number allocation can be automated in the same way.
