## __5__ Data &amp; Integrations

Your data is yours. Export any of it at any time in JSON, CSV, or PDF — nothing is held behind a paywall — and build on it through our API and push notifications.

Non-personal data is public by default, so athletes and coaches spot mistakes early and volunteers can do interesting things with it.

OpenTrack is connected to the bodies that run the sport: results sync out to World Athletics, European Athletics, and Tilastopaja, and rankings, athlete IDs, and history come back — with no re-keying.
