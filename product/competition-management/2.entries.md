#### Entries
OpenTrack has three entry modes, to make sure that entering your competition is streamlined:
- **Individual Entries**, for athletes entering themselves
- **Team Entries**, for team managers/coaches looking to enter and pay for lots of their athletes at once
- **Relay Entries**, to allow paying for a relay team on its own, without naming athletes — no more declaring a team months in advance!

For entries outside of OpenTrack, bulk imports can be done with ease, or individual competitors can be added directly to a competition as and when needed.
