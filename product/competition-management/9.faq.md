## __9__ Frequently Asked Questions

</div>

<div class="accordion faq" id="cms-faq" data-aos="fade-up">

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-1" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-1" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-1">Does OpenTrack fully replace Hy-Tek Meet Manager?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-1" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-1" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS offers a fully-featured replacement for Hy-Tek Meet Manager, with many competition organisers having smoothly made the switch already, including Scottish Athletics.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-2" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-2" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-2">Does OpenTrack integrate with FinishLynx?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-2" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-2" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates fully with FinishLynx, producing the .evt and .ppl files necessary for the smooth running of your competition.</p>
        <p>FinishLynx's autosave functionality, combined with OpenTrack's uploader, means that finished results files can go from your photofinish computer to the phones of spectators in under a second.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-3" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-3" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-3">Does OpenTrack integrate with TimeTronics/MacFinish?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-3" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-3" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates fully with TimeTronics/Macfinish, providing .par files to ensure you can capture and read races quickly and accurately.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-4" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-4" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-4">Does OpenTrack integrate with SwissTiming?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-4" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-4" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates fully with SwissTiming, exporting the concat.txt and startlist.csv files it needs to build your event, then reading the .cl and .hw7 result files it produces straight back into your competition.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-5" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-5" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-5">Does OpenTrack integrate with Alge Timing?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-5" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-5" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates fully with Alge Timing. It generates the competition.meetxml file for import into your Alge system, and reads the heatresultxml result files back in heat by heat, so times and wind readings flow into your competition automatically as each race is saved.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-6" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-6" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-6">Does OpenTrack integrate with RaceResult?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-6" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-6" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates fully with RaceResult, connecting directly to the RaceResult feed, matching transponder chips to bib numbers, so splits and finish times appear on OpenTrack while the race is still running.</p>
        <p>This makes it well suited to road, cross country, and chip-timed track events, where results reach spectators' phones within moments of each athlete crossing the line.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-7" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-7" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-7">Does OpenTrack integrate with RaceTec?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-7" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-7" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS integrates with RaceTec, pulling live results and split times from RaceTec's results feed and matching them to your competition by bib number. Chip-timed distance races populate on OpenTrack as the reads come in, with no manual export needed.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-8" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-8" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-8">Does OpenTrack integrate with MyLaps?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-8" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-8" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>OpenTrack's CMS imports results from MyLaps-timed events. Export the classifications file from MyLaps or Sporthive, upload it against the relevant race, and OpenTrack matches each finisher to their entry by bib number, bringing in places, times and splits.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-9" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-9" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-9">Can I email competitors from within OpenTrack?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-9" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-9" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>Yes. OpenTrack's CMS has a built-in mailing tool, so you can message everyone entered into a competition without exporting addresses to a separate mailing service.</p>
        <p>Messages can be personalised for each recipient's name, club, events, and payment status, saved as drafts, and test-sent to yourself before they go out. You can also mail a remote check-in link so you know who intends to compete before seeding.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-10" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-10" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-10">Can competitors enter and pay online?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-10" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-10" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>Yes. OpenTrack's CMS takes online entries and payments directly, with card processing through Stripe paid straight into the organiser's own account. Entry fees, per-event pricing, and discounts are all configured per competition.</p>
        <p>Invoices and payment confirmations are generated automatically and can be emailed to entrants and clubs.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-11" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-11" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-11">Is OpenTrack available in languages other than English?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-11" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-11" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>Yes. OpenTrack's interface is translated or partially translated into more than twenty languages, including French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Romanian, Swedish, Norwegian, Greek, Bulgarian, Russian, Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian, Albanian, Estonian, Macedonian, Belarusian and Arabic, with right-to-left and non-Latin scripts supported.</p>
        <p>If your language is not yet supported, setting up for translation is trivial.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="faq-item">
    <h3 class="faq-q-head">
      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-12" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-12" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-12">Does OpenTrack have an API?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-12" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-12" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>Yes. OpenTrack has a REST API for reading competition data — events, start lists, results and competitors — so federations and third parties can pull structured data into their own systems.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>

      <button class="faq-q" id="faq-h-13" type="button" data-toggle="collapse" data-target="#faq-c-13" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="faq-c-13">Does OpenTrack work for cross-country and road races?</button>
    </h3>
    <div id="faq-c-13" class="collapse" aria-labelledby="faq-h-13" data-parent="#cms-faq">
      <div class="faq-a">
        <p>Yes. Alongside track and field, OpenTrack's CMS runs cross country and road races, taking chip-timed results and splits from timing providers and rolling individual and team results into league standings.</p>
      </div>
    </div>
  </div>
