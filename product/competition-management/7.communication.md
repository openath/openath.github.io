## __7__ Communication

OpenTrack's built-in mailing functionality lets you keep your competitors in the loop without leaving the CMS, sending messages straight to the people entered in your competition.

#### Targeted recipients
Pick exactly who hears from you, whether everyone entered or those last few holdouts who haven't completed their entries yet. The mailing list is always shown before
you send, so you know who's included.

#### Personalised messages
You can personalise your emails with names, club details, events entered, and much more — if you need to spend a few days working on a draft you can save it and come back later, with the ability to send a test to yourself before it goes out.

#### Chase entries and check-ins
Send payment reminders to unpaid entrants, or mail a check-in link ahead of seeding so you can see who actually intends to compete.
