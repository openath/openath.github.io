## __5__ Results & Scoring

#### Live results
OpenTrack displays results live as soon as they are available — our wide range of integrations mean that the CMS can collect track, field, road, or cross-country results instantly.

If you're not quite ready to integrate your results, OpenTrack's live recording and editing screens allow input directly into the CMS from a phone, tablet, or laptop.

#### Team and combined events scoring
You name it, we can score it! We haven't yet met a scoring system we don't like, and there are dozens and dozens on offer and ready to go within OpenTrack

If you have something niche or novel we're more than happy to discuss how we can make it work!

#### Rankings and records
When your competition wraps up, results don't just need to sit on OpenTrack. With integrated outflows to World Athletics, European Athletics, and Tilastopaja, and downloads for instant upload to the UK's Power of 10, there's no re-keying of data — just frictionless processes getting your results where they need to be.
