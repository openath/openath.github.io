Athletics, Connected

Working with European Athletics and the World Wide Web Consortium, we have developed the open standard for the interchange of data in athletics. This common language allows the athletics world to communicate simply and meeting organisers to display their results to the world.

Competition Management System (CMS)

If you want to run an entire athletics meeting from first entry to final result, then our system has been used and validated at all levels, from the World Athletics Continental Tour down to local open meetings. It brings together Entries, Results Capture and Virtual Racing in one platform.
